Build a Discord bot for a group of friends who play Dungeons & Dragons together and like to listen to music while they play. Put everything for it in this folder.

What the bot should do

Dice rolling
- Players can ask the bot to roll dice the way D&D players write them, for example "one twenty-sided die", "two six-sided dice plus three", "roll four six-sided dice and keep the best three", or rolling with advantage or disadvantage.
- The bot shows each individual die result as well as the total, so everyone at the table can see the roll was fair.
- If someone types a roll that doesn't make sense, or asks for an absurd number of dice, the bot explains the problem politely instead of breaking.

Music
- Someone in a voice channel can ask the bot to join, play a song from a link, and leave again.
- Songs requested while something is already playing go into a queue.
- People can skip the current song, pause and resume, see what is playing now, see what is coming up, shuffle the queue, clear it, remove a single song from it, and turn on repeat for one song or for the whole queue.
- Each Discord server the bot is in has its own separate queue.
- The queue should not grow without limit.

Quality expectations
- The owner should be able to maintain and extend this later without help: keep it well organized, clearly named, and explained.
- Include a short guide for the owner explaining what the bot does, how to set it up, how to start it, and how to check that it works.
- The bot's private access key must be supplied by the owner when the bot is started, never written into the project.
- Include automated checks that prove the dice rolling and the queue behave correctly, and make sure they all pass before you finish.

When you are done, tell me what you built, how it is organized, and what the automated checks showed.
